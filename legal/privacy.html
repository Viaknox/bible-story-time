Privacy Policy: Bible Story Time

  • Skill: Bible Story Time (an Alexa skill)
  • Publisher: Viaknox Holding LLC, a North Carolina limited liability company
  • Contact: connect@viaknox.com
  • Effective date: November 7, 2026

This policy explains what Bible Story Time keeps about you, why, and for how long. We wrote it in plain words.

What the skill does

Bible Story Time plays short Bible stories for families. Stories are written ahead of time. They are drafted with the help of AI and reviewed by a person before they go into the skill. No AI runs while you listen. Bible verses come from the World English Bible, which is in the public domain.

What we keep

What Why Where in the skill
Your Alexa user ID Amazon gives each account a random ID for this skill. It is not your name. We use it to know which household is listening. Households table
The current month and your free-story count Free use is 10 stories a month. We count them so we know when you reach 10. The count starts over each month. Households table
Whether you pay Amazon tells the skill if you have the subscription. We store yes or no. Households table
Family access request (only if you ask for it) If you ask for free family access, we keep the first name you say, your Amazon first name if you allowed Alexa to share it, the time you asked, and whether we approved or declined it. Households table
Story and topic counts We count how often each story plays and how often a topic is asked for, such as "being brave." These counts are not tied to you. They help us choose what to write next. Stories and Tags tables

Short-term notes, like "waiting for a yes," live only during one Alexa session. They are not saved.

Family access email

If you ask for family access, the skill sends one email to the publisher. It holds the first name you said, your Amazon first name (if shared), the time, and the last 6 characters of a scrambled code made from your Alexa user ID. It has approve and reject links that stop working after 14 days. We use Amazon Simple Email Service to send it.

We only read your Amazon first name if you turned on that permission in the Alexa app. You can turn it off at any time.

What we do not keep

  • Voice recordings. Alexa hears your voice and turns it into text. Amazon handles that, not us. We never get or keep the recordings. Amazon's own privacy notice covers them.
  • Payment details. Amazon handles all payments. We never see your card or bank details.
  • Your full name, address, phone, or exact location.
  • Ads or tracking. The skill has no ads. We do not sell, rent, or share your data for marketing.

Who else handles data

Who What for
Amazon (Alexa) Runs the skill, hears your voice, handles purchases
Amazon Web Services Stores our tables and story audio, sends the family email

Story audio is stored in a private storage bucket. The skill makes a link that works for 15 minutes so your device can play it. The audio holds only the story, nothing about you.

We may share data if the law requires it.

Where data is kept

All data is kept in Amazon Web Services in the United States (us-east-1, Northern Virginia). The skill code runs on Amazon's Alexa hosting.

How long we keep it

Data How long
Household row (user ID, month, count, paid status, family request) We keep this record while the skill is in use and until you ask us to delete it. Disabling the skill does not automatically delete the record. Email connect@viaknox.com to request deletion.
Story and topic counts As long as the skill runs. They are not tied to anyone.
Family request email In the publisher's mailbox until you ask us to delete it. Email connect@viaknox.com to request deletion.
Service logs Amazon keeps technical logs. Retention varies by log; some logs have no automatic expiration configured.

How to delete your data

Email connect@viaknox.com and ask us to delete your data. Tell us the first name you used if you asked for family access. We will delete your household record when you ask, after identifying the record associated with your request.

Disabling the skill in the Alexa app stops all new data.

Children

Bible Story Time is made for families, with stories for ages 4 and up. An adult owns the Amazon account and turns on the skill. A child may listen with the family.

We do not ask children for personal details. The only spoken detail we may keep is a first name, and only when someone asks for family access. That step is meant for a grown-up.

If you believe a child gave us personal details, email us and we will delete them.

Your choices and rights

You can ask what data we have about you, ask us to fix it, or ask us to delete it. Email us.

Security

We keep data in private, encrypted storage. Only the skill and the publisher can reach it. No system is perfect, but we work to keep your data safe.
