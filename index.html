Bible Story Time

Coming soon for Alexa.

Snuggle up for a Bible story together.

Bible Story Time brings short Bible stories to families with children ages 4 and up. A warm cast of voices helps bring each story to life.

📖 A little story. A lasting lesson.

Each story includes one Bible verse from the World English Bible Updated (WEBU). The verse and its reference are read aloud. Each story shares a simple lesson about God's love.

Every story is checked against our Story Standard before release. The standard covers Bible accuracy, gentle language, family continuity and words young children can understand.

🏡 Meet the family

Mia, Sam and Lily are siblings. Their parents are Dad Ben and Mom Cara. Nana Ruth, Grandpa Joe, Papa Ray and Grandma Nell join them, along with other familiar voices. Their dogs, Buddy and Pip, are part of the family too.

💬 Try saying

When the skill is available, start with:

“Alexa, open Bible Story Time.”

Then try:

  • “Tell me a story.”
  • “Read us a story.”
  • “Another story.”
  • “Tell me about unlimited stories.”

🔒 Your family's privacy

The skill keeps an Alexa user ID, monthly story counts and subscription status. If you ask for family access, it also keeps details of that request, including a first name. It does not receive or keep your voice recordings or payment details. There are no ads, and we do not sell your data for marketing.
